# OVERHEAT

**Go faster. Get hotter. Push beyond your limits.**

Overheat is a fast-paced movement game built on GoldSrc, focused on extreme speed, aggressive combat, and managing your own heat.

Push your movement to the limit and watch your surroundings change as your body overheats. Your vision turns orange, flames begin to form around you, and pushing any further means starting over. Find water or use a fire extinguisher to cool down before things get out of control.

But speed isn't just a risk. It's a weapon.

Build up **Pure Rage** by reaching extreme speeds and taking down NPCs. Once unleashed, this ultimate ability lets you push even further, granting increased movement speed and reduced incoming damage while you tear through anything in your way.

### Core Features
- **High-speed movement** — Push your speed further and further.
- **Heat system** — Overheating escalates until you go up in flames.
- **Environmental cooling** — Use water and fire extinguishers to cool down.
- **Pure Rage** — Build your ultimate through extreme movement and NPC kills.
- **Aggressive combat** — Turn momentum into destruction.
- **GoldSrc-powered** — Built around the classic engine behind legendary FPS games.

**The only limit is how much heat you can handle.**
